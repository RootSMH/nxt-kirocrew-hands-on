### Summary

This English version is provided only so you can understand the meeting details — the meeting itself and all instructions on the day are conducted in Korean. Please bring your personal laptop and charger; Wi-Fi is available at the venue. If you would like to join, leave your name in the department group chat by 14 October 2026, 18:00 Korea time — there is no separate application link.
