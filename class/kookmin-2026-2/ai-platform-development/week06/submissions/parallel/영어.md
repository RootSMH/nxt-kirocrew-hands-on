# Department AI Hands-On Practice Meetup

## Summary

We are holding a hands-on practice meetup where department friends get to use AI directly together. The capacity is 12 people, and students who are new to AI hands-on practice are also welcome to join. This English version is provided only so you can understand the meeting details — the meeting itself and all instructions on the day are conducted in Korean. The meetup runs on 16 October 2026 from 18:30 to 20:00 Korea time (Asia/Seoul), at the Student Union Building meeting room, with no fee. Please bring your personal laptop and charger; Wi-Fi is available at the venue. If you would like to join, leave your name in the department group chat by 14 October 2026, 18:00 Korea time — there is no separate application link.
